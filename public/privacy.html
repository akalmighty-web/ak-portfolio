Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This is the personal portfolio of Ananth Krishnan. It is a showcase of my work: there are no accounts, no sign-ups and no forms, and I don't sell or share anyone's data.

What the site stores

The site uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising or tracking tools. It keeps two small settings in your own browser's local storage, which never leave your device:

  • whether you turned the site's sound effects on or off;
  • which hidden easter eggs you have found.

You can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Embedded videos

Some project pages include YouTube videos. They are embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube only stores information once you play a video, under Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

Like any website, the hosting provider (Vercel) receives standard technical information when a page is loaded, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure. See Vercel's privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Links to Behance, ArtStation, Instagram and LinkedIn take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.

Contact

If you email me, I use your address and message only to reply to you. Questions about this policy: 25akalmighty@gmail.com.
